The brief, one paragraph

Material is a venture studio and embedded operator team. The brand should read as the smartest person at the table who also happens to be the most approachable — warm, authoritative, direct. Editorial restraint, generous whitespace, a single signature green, and a serif display voice that signals craft without nostalgia.

How to read this page

The same five artifacts from the Brand Directions format — wordmark, color, type, hero, component fragment — rendered in Material's actual system. Plus the full component library behind the site: pillars, mode cards, case studies, buttons, CTA, marquee. One page, every primitive, every combination.
