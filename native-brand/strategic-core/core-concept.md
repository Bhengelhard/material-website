## What Native is

Native is a studio and embedded operator team that helps companies operate AI-Native. We work with founders and operators two ways:

- **Build with us** — a venture studio that builds new AI-Native companies alongside industry-native operators.
- **Modernize & scale** — an embedded operator team that brings strategy, structure, and systems into growing businesses, then builds toward an AI-Native operating model at the pace the company can absorb.

One team. One playbook. Two ways in.

---

## What Native does

We turn the messy middle of building a company — positioning, brand, product, GTM, ops — into a running system. Humans lead strategy. AI runs execution. Experts shape review. Everything downstream reads from the same strategic core.

### For Build with us

Native partners with industry-native operators to launch new companies. The founder brings the domain, distribution, or conviction. Native brings:

- The strategic core (positioning, ICP, narrative, brand).
- The minimum loveable product or service, with AI baked in where it makes the experience sharper.
- The traction channels, wired up as skills and agents.
- The weekly reporting and feedback loop that keeps the system getting sharper.
- A full-time CEO placement at spinout, with Native remaining on the cap table and in the boardroom.

### For Modernize & scale

Native embeds into growing 7–9 figure businesses as the operator team that builds strategy into the company. The end state is AI-Native. The path is embedded:

1. Strategy first — we rewrite the strategic core with the leadership team.
2. Structure next — org shape, roles, and the systems of record.
3. Systems last — AI-Native skills and agents, layered in where the business is ready.

We don't ship slide decks and leave. We embed until the playbook is running itself.

## Why Native exists

Most companies know AI is the single biggest lever of the decade. Very few have an operator team that can actually *install* it — into strategy, into brand, into GTM, into the day-to-day of the business — without rip-and-replace.

Consultancies deliver recommendations. Agencies deliver assets. Dev shops deliver code. None of them deliver the thing a founder or CEO actually needs: a running, AI-Native operating system with a team behind it.

Native fills that gap. We are operators who have built, run, scaled, and exited companies. We work natively inside the business, not over the wall. And we work AI-Native by default, because that's where the advantage is now.
