# positioning.md

> How Native frames itself inside the landscape. Our place in the market and our claim on it.

---

## Positioning statement

**For founders and operators building or scaling growth-stage companies, Native is the embedded operator team that installs an AI-Native operating model — strategy, brand, product, GTM, and the systems underneath — into the business from day one. Unlike consultancies that hand over decks, agencies that hand over artifacts, or dev shops that hand over code, Native operates from inside the company, owning the outcome end-to-end.**

---

## The one-sentence version

Native is the operator team that builds AI-Native companies — and makes the ones you already run native too.

---

## Category

**Category label:** Operator-led AI-Native company builder.

We don't claim a new category with a made-up name. We describe what we are in the language a buyer already uses:

- *Operator-led* — because we've sat in the seats, not observed them from outside.
- *AI-Native* — because AI runs execution, humans and experts run strategy and review, by default.
- *Company builder* — because we build the whole thing, not a slice.

---

## The two-mode positioning

Native has two front doors. The core is the same. The emphasis shifts.

### Build with us — positioning

**Native is a venture studio for industry-native operators launching AI-Native companies.**

- *Industry-native* → we work with operators who come from inside the industry they're attacking. Domain, distribution, or conviction is the ante.
- *AI-Native* → the company is designed AI-Native from day zero. Strategic core, agents, skills, and feedback loop are there at spinout, not bolted on later.
- *Studio* → shared cap structure, full-time CEO placement at spinout, Native remains on the cap table and in the boardroom.

**The pitch:** You bring the domain, distribution, or conviction. We bring the team, the playbook, and the systems. We build it with you, then spin it out with a full-time CEO.

### Modernize & scale — positioning

**Native is the embedded operator team that brings strategy, structure, and systems into growing 7–9 figure businesses — and takes them AI-Native at the pace the business can absorb.**

- *Embedded* → we sit inside the company, not across the table. The team treats us like internal operators, not vendors.
- *Strategy, structure, systems* → in that order. Strategic core first. Org and roles next. AI-Native systems last, installed where the business is ready, not where the hype is loudest.
- *At the pace the business can absorb* → AI-Native is the destination. Very few 7–9 figure companies are ready for full-stack day one. We meet them where they are and take them there.

**The pitch:** Most growing companies know they should be AI-Native. Very few have the operator team to get there without breaking what's already working. We embed, rebuild the core, and take you native.

## What we claim

1. **Strategy is the edge, not the AI.** The AI is commodity. The strategic core the AI is built on is not. We start every engagement by writing or rewriting that core.
2. **Installation beats recommendation.** We don't leave. We embed until the playbook is running on its own.
3. **One team, full stack.** Brand, GTM, product, and systems from one operator team, not five vendors trying to coordinate.
4. **The order is the product.** Follow the five phases in order and the system compounds. Skip one and it breaks. We are the team that enforces the order.
5. **Humans lead. AI executes. Experts review.** The operating model is a deliberate loop, not a pile of tools.

---

## What we do not claim

- We are not the cheapest. We are not trying to be.
- We are not the fastest to "go live." We are the fastest to a company that keeps getting sharper.
- We do not promise AI does it all. Humans do the strategy. Experts do the review. AI does the execution in between. If a buyer wants to remove any of the three, we're not the right team.

---

## Proof pillars

- **Operator credentials** — built by operators from Wharton, Harvard, Stanford, Y Combinator, Palantir, Uber, PwC, and the trenches of companies we've built, run, and exited.
- **One playbook, two modes** — the same strategic core, brand system, and agent stack powers both Build with us and Modernize & scale.
- **The five-phase playbook** — publicly written out. No black box. If a buyer wants to self-serve, we encourage it. Most don't, because the order is hard to hold.
- **Embedded engagements, not reports** — case studies of companies where we stayed in long enough for the system to compound.

## Positioning guardrails (do / don't)

**Do:**
- Talk about the operating system, the playbook, and the order.
- Use *AI-Native* and *embedded* deliberately. They carry the positioning.
- Lead with outcomes the company can feel — velocity, sharpness, compounding.

**Don't:**
- Lead with tools (Claude, Cowork, Cursor, etc.). Tools are an implementation detail.
- Sound like a consultancy. We don't recommend; we install.
- Sound like an agency. We don't deliver artifacts; we build the system that generates them.
- Over-promise autonomy. The loop always has humans and experts in it.
