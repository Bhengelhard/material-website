Variant 01·A · Letterpress

The original. Set in metal, printed on paper.

Direction 01 sharpened. We kept the palette and the voice — Playfair Display set lightly, Inter for the daily work, a brass period after the wordmark — and added what was missing: a real paper grain on every surface, drop caps in the longer reads, a Scotch rule between sections, and a Standard monogram for marks where the wordmark won't fit. The trusted advisor in the room — quiet, confident, and unmistakably hand-set.

01C
Variant 01·C · Atelier

The studio. Hairline, italic, breathing.

The same family at couture weight. Playfair light italic does the heavy lifting; Inter steps back to a quieter 300. Hairline rules instead of Scotch. A pearl paper that's almost white, sage and pale brass instead of forest and brass. More whitespace. A diamond glyph in place of the section mark. For the buyer who wants the editorial DNA — but at the temperature of a fashion house, not a firm.
