Minted Signals · Design directions

Three readings of the same brand.

Each direction started as a random string of digits, read like a signal. The digits never appear. The shape of each one does.

A
Ridgeline
High plateaus, sudden floors, then it settles. Dark and dense at the top, quiet stone by the end. A strip chart draws a live ridge under the headline. Film grain, a blueprint grid, and a light that follows the cursor.

B
Pulse
A climb, a reset, then a heartbeat. Light and off-center. A field of dots that pings outward on a rhythm and wherever you click. The headline mints itself from noise on load. A slow ticker with the local time.
Open
C
Plateau
Sustained, smooth, then very quiet. One long gradient from stone to forest across the whole page. Enormous words, tiny labels, nothing in between. A dithered band, a line that tracks the real time of day, and a flashlight at the bottom.
