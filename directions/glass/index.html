Directions

Green, four ways.

The hero has never been a flat colour: it is a wash of green with soft pools of light blurred into it, like thick glass. Everywhere else on the site the green was a straight fill. Each of these puts the hero's glass on every green surface, and each takes a different view on colour and motion. Open one and scroll the whole thing; the bar at the bottom moves you between them.

01Glass

The hero's frosted green, copied onto every green surface: the close band, the two call-to-action cards, the service cards, the menu, the 404. Same forest colour as today. The only thing that moves is the hero, as slowly as it does now.

The safe one · nothing but the surfaces change
02Deep

The same glass, mixed darker. Forest is a mid green with a lot of grey in it; this is a deep emerald, less light and more colour. The pools are pitched brighter so the glass still glows against the darker ground. Photographs and accents come down to match.

Darker, but greener, not blacker
03Drift

Glass everywhere, and the hero's light really travels. Three pools, each on its own clock, each drawn bigger and walked further, so the green is never the same twice. Below the fold nothing moves, so the motion stays a welcome rather than a tic that follows you down the page.

Motion in the hero only
04Pane

One sheet of green behind the whole site, pinned to the window. Every green surface is a window cut into that one sheet, so each shows a different part of the same wash, and scrolling slides the green through them. On top of that the sheet breathes on one slow clock, so everything brightens and dims together.

Motion everywhere, but one motion, not four

What to look for. Scroll to the bottom of each one. The close band and the two “Your company, next.” cards are where the green used to be a straight fill, so they show the change most. The service cards and the work pictures changed too, but there the green sits over a photograph, so it is quieter. On a phone, option 4 falls back to option 1, because a phone cannot hold a background still.
