How we build

How we build a company together, from first conversation to spinout.

Every venture moves through the same five stages together. We run it side by side with you, then recruit a full-time CEO and oversee them after the business spins out.

01

Explore & validate the concept

We dig into the idea together. Who the customer is, why now, what would have to be true for this to work, and what the first twelve months could actually look like.

02

Structure the partnership

If both sides want to move forward, we agree on economics, equity, roles, and scope. Clear enough to hold up, flexible enough to learn as the venture takes shape.

03

Build the strategy and materials

Positioning, brand, and the first set of things the market sees, including the website, financial model, customer pitch deck, and investor pitch deck, all built off one coherent story.

04

Ship the minimum loveable product or service

The thing customers actually pay for, delivered by a real team on real systems. A working product or service the market is ready to buy, built to be loved by its first users.

05

Recruit the CEO and spin the company out

We find and install the full-time CEO, spin the venture out, and continue to oversee the CEO to help the business keep driving forward after launch.
