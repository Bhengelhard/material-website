What this is

An embedded team of operators, working the way your team works.

When CEOs hire Material, they're hiring builders who've run and scaled companies. We come in alongside your team, own a specific outcome, and leave your business stronger than we found it.

“You do not rise to the level of your goals. You fall to the level of your systems.” — James Clear

Strategy is the clarity of where you're going and why. Positioning, ideal customer, offer, pricing — the thesis your whole company runs on.

Structure is how your company is organized to execute on that strategy. Team shape, roles, process, decisions, cadence.

Systems are the infrastructure that lets a small team operate like a big one. CRM, data, reporting, automation, and AI, all working together.

Most growing companies are strong in one and stretched thin across the other two. We find the gap and help you rebuild it, in the right order.

How we engage

We embed. We build the strategy, then we build it into the business.

We don't do one-off strategy projects. Every Material engagement starts with strategy, then brings that strategy to life inside your company, helping you turn the vision you have for the business into something real and scaled.

01 — Always

Strategy

Every engagement begins here. Clear positioning, ideal customer, offer, pricing, and a sharpened story for where the business is going. This is the foundation the rest of the work builds from.

  • Positioning and narrative
  • Ideal customer and segmentation
  • Offer and pricing design
  • Financial and KPI framework
  • Growth roadmap with clear priorities
02 — Bringing it to market

Go-to-market

Turning the strategy into something customers feel. Brand, website, sales materials, and the channels that bring new revenue in. For companies where growth has gone quiet and the playbook needs a refresh.

  • Brand and messaging refresh
  • Website and sales assets
  • Outbound, partnerships, and channels
  • Content, SEO, and paid media
  • Revenue operations and reporting
03 — Bringing it to the back end

Systems & operations

Turning the strategy into how the business actually runs. The systems, reporting, and automation that help a growing team operate with the clarity and speed of a much bigger one.

  • CRM and sales system rebuilds
  • Data infrastructure
  • Reporting and analytics
  • Automation and AI agents
  • Process and team design

Strategy and execution under the same roof, with one team that sees your business end to end.

How it works

Diagnose. Prioritize. Embed. Execute.

01

Diagnose

A focused look at where the business is and where the biggest opportunities are sitting. Spending time with you, your team, your customers, and your numbers to understand where growth is stuck and why.

02

Prioritize

We agree on the outcomes that matter most, the order to tackle them in, and the investment it'll take. Clear enough to keep us accountable, flexible enough to adjust as the work reveals what it needs.

03

Embed

A strategic operator joins your team and becomes your single point of contact. They learn your business, your market, and your customers, and they own the outcomes we committed to together.

04

Execute

Strategists, brand and product designers, engineers, and systems builders working in step with your team. Real work ships every week, and progress is visible in the business, not just the slides.

05

Hand off

KPIs tracked weekly. Playbooks documented. Your in-house team owns the system going forward. We stay on only as long as we're adding real value, not a dollar longer.
